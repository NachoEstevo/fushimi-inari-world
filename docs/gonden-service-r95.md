## Shinsenjo: independent grade fields, not a remapping fix

The existing builder places a mapped 417.8838455m² irregular compound on a **single floor sampled outside its ring**: approach `[65.326,20.483]`, just north of the mapped Z minimum 21.674. Its floor 44.122m is a composed public-court datum. The raw source DEM at that very approach is **48.000633m**. Thus nearly 3.88m of the reported mismatch exists before crossing the compound: choosing the approach sample silently transfers a locally lowered court height to an ungraded building footprint.

`integration.mjs`'s `ground` blends local legacy anchor/court grades against source DEM. Shinsenjo has no legacy spatial anchor and its new builder already uses mapped world X/Z. Moving its anchor cannot fix this mapped-footprint candidate. The transition from lowered public court to raw DEM and the building floor are different ownership decisions.

The new `auditShinsenjoGrade(features, renderedTerrainPositions, floor)` intersects **actual triangle planes with the exact concave footprint**, not its bounding box. It divides the candidate's estimated roof at both ridges and valley before comparing heights, reports a concrete maximum-intrusion witness, rejects incomplete/overlapping coverage, and changes no terrain. Run it against the final composed triangle array **before** Shinsenjo subtraction; it always withholds historical floor-registration acceptance.

Separate raw-DEM diagnostic, 1m triangulation, render offset -0.06m, floor 44.122m: footprint covered area 417.8838455000016m²; ground range 47.5329448–50.176844m; maximum terrain above floor 6.054844m; candidate roof intrusion 1.563064m at X90.940751/Z32.7 (terrain50.174564m, roof48.6115m). These are **source-DEM diagnostics**, not a claim to have rerun final assembly. Prior R93 composed maximum 50.164m remains separately documented.

Neither raw DEM 48.000633m nor composed approach 44.122m is a surveyed floor. Raising the compound to the raw approach would create a roughly 3.88m disconnect to the composed public stub; raising the roof hides the discrepancy; exact interior subtraction still leaves an exterior bank above the roof. A source-registered building datum plus a bounded courtyard/retaining interface must be resolved together. Preserve the disabled candidate until then.
