Local review copies are in `outputs/r96-romon/gehaiden-*.jpg`.

The long elevation clearly exposes a broad weathered timber stage behind the red perimeter rail, and a dark timber interior underside. The source builder instead makes the entire floor from vermilion material and gives its full rectangular mid-beam slab a vermilion underside. Both oblique and end photographs resolve indigo blue woven headers and vertical tapes on the reed blinds. R95 correctly opens the reeds but represents these borders in pale gray.

Existing pale small chevrons remain as simplified woven motifs. Detailed brocade patterns are unresolved. This is a broad surface/architectural correction, not a new building or invented offering apparatus.
