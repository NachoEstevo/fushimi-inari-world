The JR station is **Inari on the JR Nara Line**, distinct from **Keihan Fushimi-Inari** further west. The chosen train comes from Kyoto, stops on P2 and travels approximately southeast in scene coordinates `[+0.29190,0,+0.95645]`. Its open doorway faces east toward P2. Egress proceeds down the modeled internal stair to the gates, out east, then north along the public frontage toward the pre-existing Omotesando crossing. No railway crossing or footbridge is needed for this arrival.

## Canonical contract and inference

`inari-station-arrival-spec.mjs` is the common source for dimensions, rail frame, surfaces, stop, poses and route. Units are meters; X east, Y up, Z south; geographic origin 34.96714 N, 135.77267 E.

- Source station shell footprint: about 6.724 × 27.658 m. Roof heights/profile, columns and wall opening details are inferred from facade appearance.
- Street level is sampled from the existing Hommachi road surface. Public hall is +0.45 m (three inferred 15 cm front risers, exterior owned by the route module); P2 is another +0.90 m. Six internal risers of 15 cm connect the built landing and hall. These values are **inferred**, not railway measurements.
- Rail tangent is map-derived. The independently mapped P2 edge is intersected with the selected fourth-door rail cross-section. Its raw lateral coordinate is 2.200248973 m. The model's outer shell/frame half-width is 1.455 m (1.4 m body plus 0.055 m shell/frame allowance). The inferred lateral shift is therefore **2.200248973 − 1.455 − 0.160 = 0.585248973 m toward P2**. This produces a 16 cm modeled doorway gap without reshaping the mapped platform. The earlier fixed 0.85 m shift, calibrated to the third door, was discarded after changing the selected door. The corrected frame drives the rails, train, cameras, threshold and collider together; its source tangent remains unchanged.
- Train appearance interprets a four-car stainless steel/green JR local of the 205 family, with four door pairs per side per car. Car dimensions, interior furnishings, stop, current assignment and livery exactness are unverified. It is not advertised as an exact trainset replica.
- Canopy posts/length, two bridge attachment positions and heights are inferred. Bridges are context geometry; their stairs are not part of the supported player route. P1/P2 footprints remain sourced; platform edge colliders are navigation safety limits, not claims of physical platform fences.
- The historical east entrance is retained. Planned/ongoing west-exit works reported by Kyoto City for 2025/2026 are outside this scene. No claim of exact 2026 station state is made.
