# R96 Yotsutsuji sitting court and planted outlook

Actual pixels inspected from local `.tmp/r95-yotsu/court2013.jpg`, `front2013.jpg` and `downhill.jpg`. The first two are [2013 courtyard](https://zekkeikana.com/kyoto/fusimiku/fushimiinari/yotsutsuji13.jpg) and [covered sitting frontage](https://zekkeikana.com/kyoto/fusimiku/fushimiinari/yotsutsuji14.jpg), associated with the photographer's [17 February 2013 page](https://zekkeikana.com/kyoto/fusimiku/fushimiinari/yotsutsuji11.html). The third is [downhill view](https://cdn.japan-travel-note.com/images/00523/26.jpg), published in the previously reviewed Japan Travel Note article; photograph capture date remains unknown. Reference photographs are not runtime textures.

Five omissions visibly corroborated across those angles drive this packet: red covered timber sitting platforms; low pale tables; the pale beverage display cabinet beneath the main awning; dense low hedge/ferns and exposed bank rocks; flanking tree crowns around the descending gate and city aperture. Existing stone outlook edge and R89 retaining wall remain their current owners. No new signs, names, text, shops, skyline or terrain are introduced.

`buildYotsutsujiContextR96(root,features,ground,surfaces)` requires `buildYotsutsuji` to have run and receives the actual `makePathSurfaces` network. It imports R95's registered main west / annex north facade frames and their existing floor ownership. Root owns integration; this patch changes only the new builder/test/document. Do not pass an empty surface mock: missing networks fail closed.

The annex gets two low platforms with red cloth and pale low tables, positioned at facade distance 1.15 and 4.35 m, projection 0.64 m. Individual legs sample existing terrain +0.10 m rather than imposing a flat new floor. The 1.08 x 0.82 m platforms are appearance estimates; they represent the covered sitting frontage, not surveyed furniture positions. A simplified unlettered 1.10 x 1.30 x 0.48 m pale display cabinet is attached to main west frame at distance 3.10 m and projection 0.40 m. Colored inventory blocks are geometric approximation only. Two accepted public benches receive thin red pads over their existing tops, with no new footprint.

Six low rock/hedge/fern groups occupy the existing north-facing annex bank and opposite descent margin. Two low-slope trees at X783.8/Z-207.3 and X783.5/Z-218.2 frame the west aperture without planting across the descent. Shapes, sizes, species, counts and positions are inferred from the photographs; these are not surveyed historical botanical records. Their ground bases are 159.292 and 158.2527 m, so the foliage occupies lower-slope framing rather than adding a giant crown over the court. Geometry is opaque and static.

Every new furniture or planting candidate is tested against all actual route segments, including segment ends: clearance is route half-width + complete conservative object radius + 0.18 m. New furniture has no new collider; it lies outside protected walking envelopes, but unconstrained free exploration can intersect it. Native walking acceptance remains pending.
