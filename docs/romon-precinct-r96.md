# R96 Rōmon gallery structural packet

Actual pixels inspected: [north court gallery IMG6485](https://fushimiinari-guide.com/wp-content/uploads/IMG_6485_20210317.jpg), [rear oblique IMG6487](https://fushimiinari-guide.com/wp-content/uploads/IMG_6487_20210317.jpg), and [north end IMG6491](https://fushimiinari-guide.com/wp-content/uploads/IMG_6491_20210317.jpg), linked by the [Rōmon guide](https://fushimiinari-guide.com/rohmon/). Local copies are under `outputs/r96-romon/source-*.jpg` for review. No generated image was used as dimensional evidence.

The R71 galleries already reconstruct the open arcade, windows, outer walls, gable panels, roofs, rafters and ties. This packet adds four missing families across both existing galleries: exposed transverse frame bracket arms/capital blocks, individual broad stone column shoes, hexagonal gold wall/end framing plates, and narrow gilded end-gable verge strips with discrete bosses. Photographs support the stone shoes, open framed roof support vocabulary, hexagonal gold junction fittings and decorated outer gables. The exact bracket dimensions and arrangement, as well as all fine-fitting dimensions, are interpretation within the inherited frame. The model does not reproduce the photographed floral/animal carving.

`refineRomonPrecinctR96(scene, materials)` operates in the original Rōmon source coordinate frame. Call after successful `refineRomonWings`; preferably after the other source-frame refiners and before source-to-geographic transformation/material substitution. It declines if R71's successful report/group or structural materials are absent. It adds one group containing three static material batches. All source geometry, materials, UVs, roofs, ground, terrain and collision objects survive without modification. Repeated calls return the same report and insert no duplicate objects.

The 24 shoe footprints remain inside the closed gallery collision envelopes; 24 bracket arms stay between the two gallery column ranks. Lower openings, central public gate and court paths receive no new geometry. Existing R71 floors and inherited position/height contract remain authoritative. Dimensions are inherited or photo-inferred, **not surveyed**. No unverified extra sidecourt building has been fabricated.

Validation: two focused tests use the production GLB and actual R71 reconstruction. They verify both gallery families, expected fitting counts, static material batches, finite positions/UVs, bounds within the protected gallery envelope, unchanged every preexisting source vertex buffer, idempotence and refusal without partial insertion on missing integration requirements. Root owns the sole renderer; no new CPU/GPU render, browser review, walking QA or final material acceptance has been performed by this subtask. Historical accuracy, carving fidelity, wider precinct completeness and visual acceptance remain unverified.
