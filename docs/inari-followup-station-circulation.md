# Keihan Fushimi-Inari: station circulation and source evidence

Revision `Keihan-KH34-arrival-1`, researched and implemented 2026-10-24 against baseline `da6e6b9`. The user's supplied Street View is at the **Keihan Fushimi-Inari east entrance**, not JR Inari. The user confirmed Keihan as the default arrival and JR as a mapped alternative. These are independent station owners; the existing JR station files and route are preserved.

The required movement follows Keihan P2: arrive southbound from the Gion side, leave the train to the east, face the platform's outer wall, turn right/south, pass the separate southern gate and descend to Inari-shinmichi. The model does not invent an east opening through the wall. The entrance Street View establishes the exterior reference; the exact interior standing/door position is inferred.

## Canonical spatial contract

`keihan-station-spec.mjs` owns metres, X east / Y up / Z south, origin 34.96714 N / 135.77267 E and radius 6378137. All train, door, camera, platform and ramp positions derive from this frame.

- Source P2/P1 polygons are retained. The raw mapped rail tangent near the stop is `[-0.035218505, 0, 0.999379636]` toward the south. P2 is the east side.
- The raw mapped track and platform disagree laterally. The selected door's platform cross-section is 1.336407432 m from the raw track origin; subtracting the 1.436 m outer train half-envelope and a modeled 0.16 m gap gives a **−0.259592568 m** lateral resolution. This moves train/rail/camera/threshold together without reshaping the platform. The gap is an inference, not an operator measurement.
- The initial raw rail heading made rear cars 1–4 intersect the independently traced platform by up to 0.293 m. The final straight train and rails use the unchanged platform-edge heading `[-0.030900430, 0, 0.999522468]`, a **0.247545°** reconciliation about the selected vestibule. An additional −0.000014896 m cross correction retains the 0.16 m modeled gap across the entire formation. Raw direction/correction remain in the spec. Accepted camera positions/targets stay exactly fixed; the physical selected doorway moves about 6 mm. This explicitly resolves conflicting traces; it is not a claim of surveyed railway alignment.
- Seven car centers are 19.1 m apart. The second car from the leading south end, middle doorway, is selected. Each selected door leaf slides 0.68 m. Its 1.30 m opening is tested with opaque body/door meshes, including shoulder-height rays. Other train doors stay closed.
- The initial platform handoff XZ is `[-306.133183, -181.949376]`. Its camera target points east into the physical wall. The gate is to the player's right/south at `[-302.6, -151.4]`; the approach waypoint at `[-302.6, -155.5]` aligns the player before the fare housings.

Existing way 952687115 owns the 8.47 m paved path between them. The station does not add a second coplanar top over that path.

## Ownership and performance

`buildKeihanStation(root, materials, features, surfaces, options)` returns `built`, `group`, `spec`, `publicExit`, `trainGroup`, `thresholdGroup`, `colliders`, `closedDoorCollider`, `builtIds`, `surfaceHeight`, `setDoorOpen`, `setTrainOffset`, `setMode`, `routePoints` and `stats`. `options.spec` can supply the shared precomputed contract. Missing source prerequisites return an unbuilt result. A repeat build reuses the existing independent group.

Only successful integration may suppress the generic envelopes for ways 626177630, 626177631, 952687116 and 626177632. The builder does not mutate source features, delete legacy groups or alter UI/renderer state. JR remains separately owned. Its arrival and Keihan's use the existing sequence adapter.

Geometry uses the repository's local `staticParts` material batching. Current station/train/rails together contain **22,452 triangles, 39 meshes, 32 static colliders, 18 static walk-top triangles**, and no added light objects. Main geometry comprises 12 station, 3 rail and 9 train material batches; selected doors, signs and threshold retain their own transforms. Three wall posters share one atlas/material batch. Train/door/threshold dynamic descendants must be excluded from global static baking and visibility culling.

Walk heights use an indexed 4 m cell lookup inside a bounded station region, then a bounded selected vestibule/threshold check. No mesh raycast or station-wide triangle traversal runs per walking frame. Repeated unchanged train offsets and door fractions do not write transforms. Mode changes adjust existing emissive materials. These are bounded implementation costs, **not an FPS claim**.
