# R95 Rōmon and Gehaiden architecture repair

## Evidence inspected

- Existing `references/real_photos/09_romon_day_front.jpg`: open upper balcony, discrete gold rail plates, timber grid over upper-storey white plaster. The source has three complete red boxes and one complete gold box where the open balcony rails should be.
- Existing `references/real_photos/10_gehaiden.jpg`: two white plaster registers divided by red timber, open lower structure, amber bamboo blinds with narrow woven tapes, hanging paper shide, square-bodied iron lanterns.
- [Gehaiden guide](https://fushimiinari-guide.com/gehaiden/) and its [IMG_7366_20210324 photograph](https://fushimiinari-guide.com/wp-content/uploads/IMG_7366_20210324.jpg): photographed closeup confirms box lantern bodies, pierced faces, paper folds and reed blind vocabulary. The guide explicitly inventories four western, two southern, four eastern and two northern zodiac lanterns. The inherited builder incorrectly places six hexagonal lanterns on each of only two sides.
- [Official shrine description](https://inari.jp/vr_en/) confirms hip-and-gable cypress-bark Gehaiden and Rōmon. Existing roofs remain owned by the retained source meshes.

These images establish feature existence and broad appearance. No image is treated as a measured plan. Inherited source metre coordinates and building placement remain authoritative for this bounded repair.

## Architectural repairs

1. Rōmon: remove exactly four complete balcony slab components, replacing three with open perimeter rails and discrete gold fittings. Preserve original deck, balusters, finials, columns and roof. Complete-component bounding-box signature required before any mutation.
2. Rōmon: add the absent vertical exposed timber divisions on the upper white storey, attached to its existing faces and aligned with the inherited frame. No new white shell or enlarged building.
3. Gehaiden: divide its existing upper white plaster band into two registers and multiple bays using the photographed exposed red timber vocabulary.
4. Gehaiden: replace sixteen opaque blind boxes and sixty-four plaster ribbon boards with open reed slats, narrow woven tape details, a cord and folded paper strips. Geometry has real gaps; paper is double-sided. The exact fine brocade design is unresolved.
5. Gehaiden: replace the exclusive source lantern meshes with twelve square iron lanterns distributed 4/2/4/2 across all four sides. Frames and lattice have actual openings. Specific zodiac figurative medallions are unresolved and are not invented. Source nighttime emitting cylinders are removed; no new lights are introduced.
6. Both central roofs: actual CPU previews revealed a floating ridge and rectangular black/gold pediments protruding through a continuous hipped shell. Replace exactly 4,028 central shell triangles per building with a structurally separate curved gabled core and lower end hips. Add two pierced triangular gable lattices and curved verges, retaining source footprint, apex, eave elevations, shell thickness and ridge-end stations. Exact source component signatures remove the two old pediments and gold bars. Reattach 74 Rōmon and 68 Gehaiden source rafter stations beneath the corrected shell, eliminating the red beams that pierced the bark at hip corners. The shared exact component filter is now in `source-component-boxes.mjs`; the roof repair lives in `romon-gehaiden-roofs-r95.mjs` and is called automatically by the same API.

## Integration contract

`refineRomonGehaidenR95(gltf.scene, id, legacy.materials)` for `id === 'romon' || id === 'gehaiden'`, in the existing source-frame loading loop, **before material substitution**. For Gehaiden call after `refineGehaiden`, which continues to own its open balustrade. Romon curtain/gold/gable and Kairo modules remain independent; R95 does not edit their geometry.

All new geometry is attached to the source scene and inherits the existing rigid geographic transform. No building footprint, target, podium height, public route, collider or terrain is changed. Because this is elevated architecture attached to existing buildings, it requires no terrain resampling. All new work is statically merged by material; no per-frame update.

Gehaiden destructive preflight requires the production source counts: 1,728 blind, 2,256 bronze lantern, 240 light-core and 11,340 white triangles. Exactly 11,232 white ribbon/paper triangles must lie within the bounded source ribbon height range. All remaining original white plaster faces survive. Unknown source declines without partial removal. Repeated invocation returns the existing report.

## Validation and remaining acceptance

Production GLB tests exercise both buildings without browser rendering. They verify complete four-slab removal, unchanged original source vertex buffers, bounded central-roof replacement, existing Gehaiden balustrade preservation, sixteen blind panels, twelve lanterns, finite geometry, bounded material batches, idempotence and failure without mutation on unknown source. New roof gates verify exact plan bounds/apex, ridge contact, unchanged sampled eave datums, all rafter stations and corrupt-source rejection. Focused and adjacent Rōmon tests: 13 passed.

Actual offline review completed in Blender 5.2 with Cycles CPU (six threads, sixteen samples, 960×720). Four fixed views per building were rendered before and after: sixteen final frames under `outputs/r95-romon/`, plus `romon-comparison.jpg` and `gehaiden-comparison.jpg`. Initial four previews were inspected, roof/gable and rafter defects corrected, and all final after views inspected. The final reviews show joined ridges, exposed lattice gables, no red rafter spikes above bark, open balcony, readable plaster divisions, square lanterns around all four Gehaiden sides and open reed gaps. `visual-review.json` binds this review to module hashes. The renderer exited normally; no GPU or browser was used. Flat palette omits original texture maps and DOM-dependent Rōmon curtain equally in both stages, so these frames do not establish final material quality.

Post-review texture correction: the production GLBs' `MAT_Hiwadabuki_Bark_Roof` has base-color, normal and metallic/roughness textures; integration does not replace this material or generate UVs. The new shell therefore now supplies explicit UVs at the source's two-metre metric texture scale. Main slopes and hips share the same global plan basis, so their join stations align; thin vertical fascia uses edge/height coordinates to avoid collapsed texture triangles. The original source material object and all texture slots remain shared. Production-GLB regressions verify finite UVs for every emitted vertex, nonzero UV area on every triangle, the exact two-metre mapping on all sloped faces and material/texture identity. Six focused tests passed after this correction. Exported diagnostic GLBs were refreshed; shape, cameras and flat-color renders are unchanged. Textured browser appearance still needs root review.

Root must still inspect the integrated front, rear and side views and public walking clearances. Source dimensions, blind thickness, paper scale, detailed joint placement and lantern stations are inferred rather than surveyed. Zodiac medallions, guardian sculpture fidelity, detailed historical fabric and full precinct exactness remain unresolved. This source-backed repair is substantial architecture work; it is not a claim of complete historical reconstruction.
