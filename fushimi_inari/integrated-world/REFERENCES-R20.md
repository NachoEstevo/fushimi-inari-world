# Romon planted terraces and west landing — R20

Source: [Attractions Atlas walking tour](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FmRyJ-RtBPg&t=35s), paused at 00:35 and 00:40, compared with the previously reviewed 00:30 and 00:54 views.

Observed: a lower dark stone retaining wall, continuous clipped evergreen hedge, a second wall set back behind it, and another planted strip at the entrance level. The water pavilion stands in front of these terraces. The central stairs remain open between the two garden wings.

Implemented: two retaining levels with caps, soil beds and end walls; continuous small-leaf hedge crowns; a flat tiled west landing extending beyond the mapped stair endpoint. Existing shrubs, buildings, basin and roofs remain present. The foliage adds two instanced draws with shared geometry and no additional shadow casters.

Inferred, not surveyed: wall heights (0.96/1.62 m), setbacks, planting species and dimensions of the west landing. The video does not give an unobstructed survey of that entire side landing. The OSM west-stair alignment is retained. Do not treat these dimensions as measured reality.

Validation: walked from Temizuya up the west stairs, onto the landing and onward to the courtyard in the existing browser tab. No blocking wall or camera penetration on that traverse. One frontend sample reported 153 FPS, 5.99 ms submit time and 2,136 calls at 1205×812, DPR 1; this is not a comparative benchmark or a low-end performance guarantee. The full scene remains expensive (~12.15 million submitted triangles in that view).

Remaining: detailed facade geometry at the west courtyard, exact landing junction/paving transitions, real hedge species confirmation and measured elevations. The entrance is improved, not a completed surveyed replica.
