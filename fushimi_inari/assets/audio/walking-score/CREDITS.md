# Walking score and pond recording

These recordings are distributed under CC0 1.0. The original authors and primary license pages are retained here even where attribution is optional. No Minecraft recordings, melodies or game assets are included.

[Source](https://opengameart.org/content/first-light-particles-%E2%80%93-cc0-atmospheric-pianoambient-track) · [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). File: `first-light.ogg`. Loudness adjusted to -23 LUFS; stereo Opus 64 kbps VBR, 48 kHz. Complete composition, no remix or looping.

- **Yoiyami Core Theme – Deep Blue Ambient Piano** — Yoiyami. [Source](https://opengameart.org/content/yoiyami-core-theme-%E2%80%93-deep-blue-ambient-piano) · [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). File: `blue-hour.ogg`. Loudness adjusted to -23 LUFS; stereo Opus 64 kbps VBR, 48 kHz. Complete composition, no remix or looping.

- **Birds, wind through bamboo and a little waterstream - Japan - Binaural** — tomasloos. [Source](https://freesound.org/people/tomasloos/sounds/843520/) · [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). File: `tottori-bamboo.ogg`. Source: public high-quality preview. Excerpt 45–85 seconds; tail/head equal-power two-second crossfade makes a 38-second loop. High-pass 70 Hz, low-pass 10 kHz, -28 LUFS; stereo Opus 48 kbps VBR. Recorded in Tottori, not Fushimi Inari; used as a local acoustic interpretation around Shin-ike.

File sizes, measured durations and source/local SHA-256 hashes are in [manifest.json](manifest.json).
