`uguisu-koba-chan.ogg`: “Japanese nightingale note05”, Koba-chan,
recorded Kantō, Japan, 2005-04-07. Unmodified original Ogg.
[Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Japanese_nightingale_note05.ogg).
[CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
SHA-1: 5d0b82f9153a37a31a8439cf2bd378154e0cd998.
Playback uses gain, distance and stereo positioning; no pitch alteration.
The recording was not made at Fushimi Inari.

Uguisu is listed in the Kyoto mountaineering federation's 2020-10-11
Tofukuji–Inariyama field excursion report:
https://kyoto-gakuren.jp/w/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/ba29e800d620169b8116ef040ca73f3d.pdf
The report covers the whole excursion, not individual sound emitter locations.
The spring soundscape is an interpretation, not an on-site acoustic survey.

Breeze and occasional small chimes are synthesized. Chime placement by tea
houses is interpretive; no claim is made that those shops have wind chimes.
The original interaction bell, water and footsteps remain available.
