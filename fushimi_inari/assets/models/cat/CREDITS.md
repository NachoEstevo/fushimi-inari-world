# Mugi station cat

- Original model: **Cat**, by **byzmod3d**.
- Original author publication: [Low Poly Animal Pack (WIP), OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/low-poly-animal-pack-wip), published May 12, 2026. The author identifies the cat as complete in the May 14 comment.
- Downloaded directly from the author's attached [cat_news_0.blend](https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/cat_news_0.blend) on October 28, 2026.
- License: **CC0 1.0 Universal**, explicitly stated in the publication and attribution notice. [Public domain dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- Original source SHA-256: `f105013b2fc013dd950510e3dcba6e21b3cc6643bd3c987f7bc550e6b210bc69`.

Local adaptation retains the author's head, ears, eyes and whiskers, with muted gray/pink inner ears. The body and limbs were remodeled into a continuous seated coat with a rounded chest/abdomen, folded hindquarters and nearly vertical resting forearms. A new gently curled tail stays clear of the station seat. Paw soles retain their support plane. No image textures or lights were added. The adaptation faces -Z and is approximately 0.35 m tall. Sitting proportions were checked against [Dcoetzee's own-work photograph, released into the public domain](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tabby_kitten_sitting.jpg).

The source contains no skeleton or animation clips. The local adaptation adds a 28-joint skin with separate elbows, carpi, stifles and hocks, one blink morph target and four authored clips: an 18-second quiet idle with independent attention/ear events, a deliberate paw gesture, greeting/sniffing, and relaxed petting with separate paw adjustments. Paw inverse kinematics are baked offline; runtime only blends clips. The tail belongs to the pelvis, and an independent gaze joint keeps camera tracking separate from the authored motion. Animation and interaction lifetimes share one active clock so menus cannot cut a gesture short.

`Mugi.glb`: 524,712 bytes; `Mugi.glb.gz`: 153,209 bytes, deterministic lossless gzip transport. 6,096 triangles, 3,375 vertices, one skinned mesh primitive, one material, zero image textures. GLB SHA-256: `186f5dba16f9d5d766c0e8b025295f52157e075a0fa4369a5050d2a2b0b53d23`.
