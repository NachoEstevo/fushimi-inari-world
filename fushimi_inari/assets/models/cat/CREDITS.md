# Mugi station cat

- Original model: **Cat**, by **byzmod3d**.
- Original author publication: [Low Poly Animal Pack (WIP), OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/low-poly-animal-pack-wip), published May 12, 2026. The author identifies the cat as complete in the May 14 comment.
- Downloaded directly from the author's attached [cat_news_0.blend](https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/cat_news_0.blend) on October 28, 2026.
- License: **CC0 1.0 Universal**, explicitly stated in the publication and attribution notice. [Public domain dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- Original source SHA-256: `f105013b2fc013dd950510e3dcba6e21b3cc6643bd3c987f7bc550e6b210bc69`.

Local adaptation applies the author's mirror and body subdivision, smooths normals while preserving sharp ear and eye edges, and bakes the original flat coat colors into one vertex-color material. The head and whiskers form a separate mesh with its origin at the neck. The source contains no skeleton or animation clips; runtime applies only a small head turn. The cat faces -Z, has a 0.50 m nose-to-tail footprint, and is 0.378 m tall. Paw soles were flattened onto one support plane by at most 6 mm. No textures or lights were added.

`Mugi.glb`: 111,680 bytes; `Mugi.glb.gz`: 51,568 bytes, deterministic lossless gzip transport. 3,772 triangles, two mesh primitives, one material, zero textures. GLB SHA-256: `3b12f89ea56546c63e3593319de7d19fbb1fc338a5f71895c74d474c84d55ce2`.
